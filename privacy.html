3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Enquiries and professional correspondence: to respond to requests and communicate with business contacts. Our legal basis is legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), namely managing enquiries and professional relationships, or Article 6(1)(b) where necessary for steps requested by an individual before entering into a contract.
  • Consultancy and client administration: to prepare, perform and administer agreed services. Our legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) where the contract is with an individual, or legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) for business-to-business relationship administration.
  • Accounting and legal compliance: to meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Website operation and security: to deliver and secure the website, based on legitimate interests in maintaining a reliable and secure online presence (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), to the extent that Stanex determines the relevant processing.

Providing information through an enquiry is voluntary, but we may be unable to respond without contact details. Some information is required to enter into or administer a contract or to comply with the law.

Our hosting provider may process technical connection data for delivery and security purposes. Such processing does not necessarily involve cookies. If we introduce optional analytics, embedded third-party content or other technologies requiring consent, we will update this notice and obtain the required consent before activating them.

Providers process data in accordance with their applicable roles and agreements. Depending on the service and purpose, they may act as processors on our behalf or as independent controllers for their own legitimate purposes. We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

Some service providers may process or access personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where international transfers occur, the applicable safeguards may include an adequacy decision by the European Commission or measures such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as appropriate to the provider and transfer concerned.

You may contact us to request further information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

7. Data retention

  • General enquiries that do not become client relationships: we retain correspondence for no longer than 12 months after the last substantive communication, unless a longer period is necessary for an ongoing matter, legal obligation or legal claim.
  • Client and contractual records: we retain relevant information during the relationship and for as long as reasonably necessary thereafter to meet contractual, legal or dispute-related requirements.
  • Accounting and tax documents: documents subject to Belgian statutory retention requirements are generally kept for 10 years following the relevant taxable period, or for longer where specifically required by law.
  • Website hosting logs: technical logs handled by GitHub Pages are subject to the hosting provider's applicable retention practices; Stanex does not directly control those log-retention periods.

We periodically review records and delete or anonymise personal data when it is no longer required.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to our activities, including access controls and multi-factor authentication for our business accounts. We also rely on security features provided by Microsoft 365 and our website and domain service providers.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely risk-free. We review our security measures as our activities and processing requirements evolve.

To exercise your rights, contact us at stan@stanexstrategy.com . We handle requests in accordance with applicable GDPR requirements.

To exercise your rights, email stan@stanexstrategy.com . We normally respond within one month, subject to the extensions permitted by the GDPR. We may request information needed to verify your identity before disclosing personal data.

10. Automated decision-making

11. Complaints

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority. In Belgium, this is the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit (Belgian Data Protection Authority) .
